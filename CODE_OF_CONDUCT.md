# Community Code of Conduct / 社群行為準則

## Our standard

LingoMuse welcomes people of every background and experience level. Be respectful, constructive, patient, and mindful that language and cultural context can differ.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## 我們的準則

LingoMuse 歡迎不同背景與經驗程度的參與者。請保持尊重、建設性與耐心，並理解語言及文化脈絡可能不同。

我們不接受騷擾、歧視、威脅、蓄意公開他人私人資訊，或持續破壞討論的行為。

## Reporting / 回報

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method listed in the repository security policy. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

若遇到社群行為問題，請透過儲存庫安全政策所列的維護者聯絡方式私下回報。我們會以公平且保密的方式處理。

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this code.

專案維護者可移除違反本準則的內容，並限制相關參與權限。
